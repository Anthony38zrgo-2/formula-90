## V10-007 — Source boundaries and downstream excitation

`v10_boundary_regression` exercises five cases: consecutive steady 720-degree
cycles, low-energy motoring, high energy, changing RPM/load, and start/stop.
It records finite/bounded chamber state, firing sequence, phase-step error, and
first-difference/second-difference indicators for chamber, exhaust excitation,
runner header/acoustic pressure, collector exhaust, and master output.

The 48 kHz run passed the semantic and numerical checks. The observed firing
sequence begins `[0,5,1,6,2,7,3,8,4,9]`, phase-step error is `0.0 deg`, and
the 4-second 9000 RPM case records 3001 boundary events including the initial
event. The runtime matrix also passed at 44.1/48 kHz and 128/256/512-frame
blocks. The report is:

`reports/audio-v10/v10-007-011/boundary-regression.json`

The high-band fields are numerical excitation indicators, not a realism claim.
The source/full-scene listening comparison remains a human gate.

## V10-008 — Master saturation and zero-color bypass

`VehicleAudioEngine::new`, `set_config`, and JSON reload now apply one effective
`MasterConfig.saturation` and `MasterConfig.limiter_threshold` snapshot. The
master coloration stage is separate from the linked `StereoLimiter`:

```text
master input -> zero-transparent coloration -> linked safety limiter -> output
```

At saturation zero, coloration returns the exact input sample. The linked
limiter remains active under overload; no global headroom, layer balance, or
limiter-ceiling retune was bundled into this fix.

## V10-009 — Truthful protection diagnostics

Stage diagnostics are disabled by default and can be enabled for evidence
captures. They report master input/color/output peak and RMS, coloration delta,
effective saturation/ceiling, limiter-active samples, and positive linked
limiter gain reduction. `protection_reduction_db` now describes the linked
limiter rather than implying that the entire chain is linear.

The current probe reports, for the zero-color steady case, `coloration_delta_peak
= 0`, limiter activity `0`, and equal master-input/master-color peaks. The
explicit overload probe reports input/color peak `1.8445317`, final peak
`0.9000000`, 44 active samples, and `6.2329 dB` linked reduction. This is the

The probe artifact is:

`reports/audio-v10/v10-007-011/provenance-probes.json`

## V10-010 — Physics-driven transient response

`v10_physics_transient_capture` runs the real `CoreFacade::step_standalone`
path with `f1_2026_2008_physics.json`, then renders the audio block after each
physics tick. It records RPM/load/torque/clutch/TC/limiter/shift telemetry and
audio peak/RMS at the audio boundary. It does not force synthetic TC or
limiter flags.

Observed in the 8-second, 120 Hz capture:

- 16 shift-cut steps and 10 recovery steps, with first cut at 3.5 s and first
  recovery at 3.5667 s;
- 240 TC-active steps beginning at 6.0 s;
- no physical limiter-active step in this scenario;
- audio peak `0.57712`, RMS `0.06969`, and maximum block-peak step change
  `0.25229`.

The raw row trace and summary are:

- `reports/audio-v10/v10-007-011/physics-transient-capture.csv`
- `reports/audio-v10/v10-007-011/physics-transient-summary.json`
