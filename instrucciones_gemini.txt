1. At 1280x720 minimap is approximately 216x256 physical pixels and gauge
   approximately 352x220; neither dominates car or road view.
2. Blue player marker moves and rotates with vehicle transform.
3. Green start marker stays fixed and distinct.
4. Bootstrap test fails if vehicle binding is missing or projected map position
   remains unchanged after vehicle movement.
5. HUD remains outside WorldPresenter and unfiltered by world-only processing.
6. No physics, skybox, track, texture, or vegetation regression.
