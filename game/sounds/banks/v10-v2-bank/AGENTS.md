# v10-v2-bank — Canonical V10 engine bank

Scope: the canonical runtime V10 engine bank: manifest, source inventory, and prepared runtime assets co-located.
Consumers: the F1 2030 V10 vehicle profile and the runtime sampler.
Rules: the bank ships only the prepared runtime WAVs declared by `manifest.json` (engine `*_loop.wav`, events `*_event.wav` and `backfire_burst_*`, `limiter_cut_loop.wav`, `transmission_whine_loop.wav`). The builder and the intermediate tool keep their offline preparation records; onboard provenance tools reconstruct them with revision 12. New recordings must not be staged here. This folder carries a `.gdignore`: Godot never imports these WAVs.

The engine uses six prepared powered Ferrari F2002 Ver-D loops, three coast loops, and a separate idle loop: `engine_idle_loop.wav`, `engine_powered_low_loop.wav`, `engine_mid_on_loop.wav`, `engine_powered_medium_high_loop.wav`, `engine_powered_high_loop.wav`, `engine_powered_maximum_loop.wav`, `engine_low_off_loop.wav`, `engine_mid_off_loop.wav` and `engine_off_maximum_loop.wav`. Powered presence covers low at 4800–11500, medium at 9500–13000, medium-high at 12000–16000, high at 14500–17500, and maximum at 16500–18000 revolutions per minute. Idle remains exclusive through 4800 and fades out at 5600. Smoothstep equal-power crossfades span 4800–5600, 9500–11500, 12000–13000, 14500–16000, and 16500–17500. Medium contributes nothing at or above 13000. Existing idle, medium, and maximum gains remain preserved; the three new layers match the medium recording's 300–6000 Hz band level. Measured recording pitch determines playback references independently of authored crossfade ranges. All runtime loops use mono PCM16 at 44100 Hz with phase rotation and loop seam crossfading.

Coast playback uses `engine_low_off_loop.wav` at 4500–11500, `engine_mid_off_loop.wav` at 9500–16000, and `engine_off_maximum_loop.wav` at 14000–18000 revolutions per minute. Equal-power smoothstep transitions span 9500–11500 and 14000–16000, with measured coast level calibration anchored to the prior low-coast gain of 0.7889246022157463. Removing intermediate loops does not renormalize the low-coast volume upward.

`limiter_cut_loop.wav` is the prepared limiter runtime recording. The limiter group uses `limiter_cut_window`: playback follows the physical limiter cut intervals, with no entry event, cooldown, or audio-side revolutions threshold. Other groups retain their event behavior.

`transmission_whine_loop.wav` converts the canonical gearbox whine into a three-second loop with applied calibration. Its authored 2000 hertz mesh anchor preserves the recording's approximately 6000 hertz character at unity playback speed; it is an acoustic calibration, not a measurement of the donor vehicle's transmission. A gain of 1.5 matches the previous synthetic whine's mean level in the controlled acceleration and coast fixture while retaining the profile's `gearbox_whine_gain` of 0.137826425.

With this entry present, the sampler replaces both synthetic tones and synthetic noise with one continuous recording. Output-shaft mesh speed controls pitch, transmitted load and torque direction retain their existing volume response, and upshift recovery retains its existing modulation. The recording pauses silently at zero shaft frequency; very low speeds fade below the minimum 0.1 playback rate. Playback is limited to 4.0. Periodic, phase-aligned low-pass recording levels are prepared during bank loading and interpolated before cubic sample playback; the audio callback performs no loading or allocation. Manifests without the optional entry retain the previous synthesizer. A declared recording with invalid metadata, WAV format, frame count or hash fails initialization.

Rebuild the canon only with `tools/audio/build_canonical_v10_engine_bank.py` in offline mode. Intentional audio changes still update sources, tool revision, derived hashes, and the suite in `tools/audio/tests/` in one change.
