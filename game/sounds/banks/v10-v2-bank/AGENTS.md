# v10-v2-bank — Canonical V10 engine bank

Scope: the canonical runtime V10 engine bank: manifest, source inventory, raw sources, and derived loops and events co-located.
Consumers: the F1 2030 V10 vehicle profile and the runtime sampler.
Rules: raw names (`engine_*`, `backfire_*`, `gearup`, `geardn`, `limiter`) are sources; derived names (`*_loop`, `*_event`, `backfire_burst_*`) are runtime assets. Rebuild only with `tools/audio/build_canonical_v10_engine_bank.py`; verify with `--check`. This folder carries a `.gdignore`: Godot never imports these WAVs.

The engine uses five powered Ferrari F2002 Ver-D recordings, three coast samples, and a separate idle loop. Source mapping is `low_on.wav` to `engine_powered_low.wav`, `med_on.wav` to `engine_on_med.wav`, `med_hi_on.wav` to `engine_powered_medium_high.wav`, `hi_on.wav` to `engine_powered_high.wav`, and `max_on.wav` to `engine_on_high.wav`. Canonical source bytes match the supplied recordings. Powered presence covers low at 4800–11500, medium at 9500–13000, medium-high at 12000–16000, high at 14500–17500, and maximum at 16500–18000 revolutions per minute. Idle remains exclusive through 4800 and fades out at 5600. Smoothstep equal-power crossfades span 4800–5600, 9500–11500, 12000–13000, 14500–16000, and 16500–17500. Medium contributes nothing at or above 13000. Existing idle, medium, and maximum gains remain preserved; the three new layers match the medium recording's 300–6000 Hz band level. Measured recording pitch determines playback references independently of authored crossfade ranges. All runtime loops use mono PCM16 at 44100 Hz with phase rotation and loop seam crossfading.

Coast playback uses `engine_off_low.wav` at 4500–11500, `engine_off_med.wav` at 9500–16000, and `engine_off_maximum.wav` at 14000–18000 revolutions per minute. Equal-power smoothstep transitions span 9500–11500 and 14000–16000, with measured coast level calibration anchored to the prior low-coast gain of 0.7889246022157463. Removing intermediate loops does not renormalize the low-coast volume upward. Unselected source recordings and derived loops remain preserved on disk. Coast WAV data, measured coast pitch references, events, and trigger policy retain their prior contents.

`limiter.wav` preserves the supplied limiter recording. `limiter_cut_loop.wav` is its mono16 44100 Hz runtime derivative. The limiter group uses `limiter_cut_window`: playback follows the physical limiter cut intervals, with no entry event, cooldown, or audio-side revolutions threshold. Other groups retain their event behavior.

`transmission_whine_source.wav` preserves the supplied Ferrari F2002 Ver-D `turbo.wav` byte for byte. Builder revision 12 selects a stable three-second interior window, removes its mean, joins its ends with a 60 millisecond linear overlap, and calibrates the derived `transmission_whine_loop.wav` to a root mean square of 0.04. The loop lasts 2.94 seconds. The optional schema-1 `transmission_whine` entry records both hashes and the preparation recipe. Its authored 2000 hertz mesh anchor preserves the recording's approximately 6000 hertz character at unity playback speed; it is an acoustic calibration, not a measurement of the donor vehicle's transmission. A gain of 1.5 matches the previous synthetic whine's mean level in the controlled acceleration and coast fixture while retaining the profile's `gearbox_whine_gain` of 0.137826425.

With this entry present, the sampler replaces both synthetic tones and synthetic noise with one continuous recording. Output-shaft mesh speed controls pitch, transmitted load and torque direction retain their existing volume response, and upshift recovery retains its existing modulation. The recording pauses silently at zero shaft frequency; very low speeds fade below the minimum 0.1 playback rate. Playback is limited to 4.0. Periodic, phase-aligned low-pass recording levels are prepared during bank loading and interpolated before cubic sample playback; the audio callback performs no loading or allocation. Manifests without the optional entry retain the previous synthesizer. A declared recording with invalid metadata, WAV format, frame count or hash fails initialization.
