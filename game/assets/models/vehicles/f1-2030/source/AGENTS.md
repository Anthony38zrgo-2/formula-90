# source — 2030 authoring sources

Scope: Blender sources and texture packs behind the 2030 meshes and liveries.
Consumers: `tools/blender/` remodel, livery, and validation scripts.
Rules: sources regenerate the runtime meshes; review renders are evidence, not releases. The driver-fitted seat is regenerated with tools/blender/fit_f1_2030_driver_seat.py; driver_seat_fit_report.json records the protected geometry and animated-driver clearance checks.

The source includes VehicleAuthoring and FinalVehicleStudio scenes sharing the vehicle objects. Studio ground, lights and cameras belong only to FinalVehicleStudio and must remain outside vehicle exports. FinalVehicleRenderPreset.json is an embedded Blender text containing the saved 3840 by 2160 Cycles preset, lights and cameras. Subtle paint and carbon variations are baked into the existing base color and packed material maps by tools/blender/refine_vehicle_finishes_and_save_studio.py; the operation is intentionally guarded against repeated application. Geometry, UV coordinates, sponsor decals, wheel finishes and normal maps are preserved. Review renders use reduced settings in memory after saving the final preset.

The cockpit clearance is regenerated with tools/blender/fit_f1_2030_cockpit_clearance.py. It captures the current driver in an isolated Godot project and records source, animation and geometry hashes in cockpit_clearance_report.json. Repeating the fitting requires --source-file pointing to the original source recorded by that report, so the adjustments are applied once. The operation fits GEO_CHASSIS_BODY, GEO_CHASSIS_INTERIOR and GEO_CHASSIS_FLOOR, translates the seat and complete steering wheel assembly rearward by 100 millimeters, and extends GEO_CHASSIS_STEERCOLUM while preserving its forward anchor. The matching seated_position belongs to the vehicle scene; the driver mesh and skeleton proportions remain unchanged. Promotion requires no intersections across the animated driver samples or between the seat and surrounding cockpit meshes. Other meshes remain protected.

GEO_CHASSIS_CockpitUpperLining closes the transition between the interior walls and the authored cockpit opening, including the footwell roof. Regenerate or replace the lining with tools/blender/seal_formula_one_2030_cockpit_interior.py, supplying --source-file, a current --driver-surfaces capture and --output-directory under scratch/. Relocated source backups must retain resolvable authored texture paths. Reconstruction matches longitudinal sections, joins the side, front and rear panels and retains the complete lower contour. Only the attachment vertices of GEO_CHASSIS_INTERIOR may move laterally; its final footwell attachment vertices may rise to preserve foot clearance. Its inner and outer faces use matching triangulation and consistent outward normals, with the existing texture coordinates retained. Other meshes remain protected, the driver entry stays open, and the lining uses the existing interior carbon material. The lining skin separation is 1.5 millimeters vertically; normal thickness varies with surface slope. tools/blender/validate_formula_one_2030_cockpit_geometry.py checks both reconstructed meshes for manifold topology, winding, degenerate faces, triangle crossings and coplanar overlaps, then samples every opening and interior perimeter edge at intervals of at most one millimeter. The maximum accepted attachment distance is 0.02 millimeters. cockpit_interior_sealing_report.json records source and capture provenance, protected mesh fingerprints, attachment changes, full perimeter measurements, driver poses and the steering assembly sweep. The isolated driver capture must include the current head-motion and cockpit-camera dependencies. Promotion requires successful geometry and clearance validation and regenerates only the chassis export and its manifest records.

GEO_CHASSIS_CockpitUpperLining and GEO_CHASSIS_SEAT use smooth face shading with sharp edges above 35 degrees and explicit sharp edges between each shell skin and its thickness rim. tools/blender/shade_formula_one_2030_cockpit_surfaces.py regenerates this shading without changing vertex positions, connectivity, texture coordinates, material assignments or triangle counts. cockpit_shading_report.json records source and export validation, including preservation of split corner normals and other meshes. The chassis export retains unrelated tangent values when exporter rounding varies; the four wheel exports remain unchanged. The sealing report records the authorized seat shading update and its historical backup while retaining the unchanged geometry and clearance evidence.

The optimized lining is regenerated with tools/blender/reconstruct_formula_one_2030_cockpit_lining.py, using --source-file, --output-directory, --driver-surfaces and --maximum-deviation-millimeters 0.25. Use the texture-resolved source_before_reconstruction.blend recorded in cockpit_lining_reconstruction_report.json as the baseline; repeated optimization of an already reconstructed lining is rejected. The reconstruction modifies only GEO_CHASSIS_CockpitUpperLining, preserving every skin boundary segment, existing crease edge and the vertical shell separation. It selects local triangulation by surface deviation, preserves the continuous texture projection, and validates the complete cockpit perimeter and animated driver capture. The saved lining has 2148 triangles versus the baseline 2492. Preserved crease vertex pairs are stored on the lining object so subsequent shading preserves intentional edges even when their new face angles fall below 35 degrees. Chassis export sets explicit transformed corner normals on the lining duplicate to avoid normal drift in narrow triangles; other source meshes and the four wheel exports are preserved. cockpit_lining_reconstruction_report.json supersedes the earlier lining topology and shading measurements and records the new source and export hashes.

Chassis sponsor designs and Italian flags are flat colors in the existing red lacquer and satin carbon base color textures. They add zero triangles and have no independent decal objects, planes, thickness or normal map relief. tools/blender/convert_formula_one_2030_chassis_decals_to_textures.py regenerates this conversion from the texture-resolved source_before_flat_decal_conversion.blend recorded in chassis_flat_decal_report.json; it rejects sources already converted. Supply --source-file and --output-directory under scratch/. The conversion removes 38 historical GEO_CHASSIS_DECAL_ meshes totaling 20836 triangles, preserves retained mesh positions, topology, texture coordinates, shading and sharp edges, and copies the two recipient materials with new base color maps under textures/chassis_flat_decals/. Existing normal, roughness, metallic and occlusion maps remain unchanged. Only the chassis export is regenerated, reducing its runtime triangles from 119949 to 99113 and material primitives from 74 to 36. Wheel meshes, wheel decals and all four wheel exports remain unchanged. The report records projection samples for all 38 designs, both side and top review renders, source and export validation, and backups on D:. Earlier cockpit reports retain their historical fingerprints and link the authorized material aliases and decal removal to this report.

The subsequent reference finish rebalance is regenerated with tools/blender/rebalance_formula_one_2030_reference_finishes.py. Supply the preceding canonical source as --source-file, the historical source_before_flat_decal_conversion.blend as --decal-baseline, the four preceding runtime wheel GLBs as --wheel-baseline-directory, and --output-directory under scratch/. The operation is guarded against repeated application. It adjusts red paint, carbon color and roughness, normal intensity, and the placement of eight sponsor families on both sides. All 38 chassis designs remain flat colors in the existing baked texture coordinates. The tire sidewall band and lettering are flat color textures, with a shared rubber roughness map and no relief. Eight historical wheel decal meshes totaling 4416 triangles are removed. Retained authoring mesh positions, connectivity, smooth faces, corner normals and sharp edges remain unchanged; only tire texture coordinates and authorized materials change. The complete cockpit attachment perimeters are revalidated. reference_finish_report.json records the placements, protected geometry, projection coverage, runtime material sharing, captures, performance comparison and backups on D:.

The preceding authoring source contains higher density tire and rim meshes than the preceding runtime wheel exports. To preserve the established runtime budget and wheel appearance, 28 low density wheel surfaces are embedded as meshes with fake users in the source. Each retained wheel object refers to its surface through formula_one_2030_runtime_surface_mesh. tools/blender/export_f1_2030_v10.py exports these embedded surfaces with transformed corner normals; the tire surfaces use the new shared material and coordinates, while all other wheel surfaces retain the preceding runtime material parameters, texture pixels and coordinates. The authoring scene keeps its original geometry. Intentional later changes to wheel geometry require regenerating the corresponding embedded runtime surfaces; changing only the visible authoring mesh does not update that surface. The chassis remains 99113 runtime triangles and each wheel becomes 2748, totaling 110105 for the vehicle. SpinVisual and BrakeStatic contracts are preserved.

reference_finish_projection_receivers.json retains the 38 design-to-surface mappings used by the rebalance tool. The tool reads this tracked layout by default; --projection-receivers can select a reviewed replacement. It does not depend on a local scratch inspection file. Source and texture backups created by the finish and studio tool remain under the repository scratch/ directory.

It replaces only its own authoring_generator-tagged headrest and preserves all other vehicle meshes. tools/blender/cockpit_headrest_support_profiles.py intersects the existing lining and interior skins with radial section planes to trace continuous support curves, including the hidden rear overhangs. The outer upper edge follows the exact cockpit opening contour, while the exposed padding slopes inward below that rim and the front ends remain recessed. Outer support follows the existing chassis surfaces instead of leaving the former six millimeter attachment offset. Measured final bounds belong to cockpit_headrest_report.json.

Hidden support extending higher than its local opening edge must remain covered by the existing body. Minor driver animation clipping remains authorized: configured head contacts must stay below 12 millimeters, and other sampled driver contacts below four millimeters. The 64 head samples also include 18 extreme horizon poses already intersecting the original chassis; extremes are recorded separately and do not determine the padding silhouette. tools/blender/capture_driver_head_motion_surfaces.gd captures the production modifier and skin deformation in an isolated Godot project. Copy the current driver_head_motion_modifier.gd, cockpit_driver_motion_state.gd and cockpit_camera_configuration.gd to matching res://scripts/ paths, import the isolated project, and pass repository, driver model, camera configuration and scratch output paths as four positional arguments.
