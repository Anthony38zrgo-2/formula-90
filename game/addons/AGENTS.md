# addons — Godot editor plugins

Scope: plugins loaded by the Godot editor: the owned Formula90 bridge plus vendored third-party plugins.
Consumers: the editor and every scene in `game/scenes/`.
Rules: vendored plugins (`gdUnit4`, `gevp`) carry no AGENTS.md of their own; they are covered here and must not be patched in place.
Subfolders: each owned child folder documents itself in its own AGENTS.md.
